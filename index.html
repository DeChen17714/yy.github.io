YY Ink Studio: from craft to customer

I founded YY Ink Studio in 2025 to turn my calligraphy practice into a customer-facing creative brand. The work brings together handmade products, booth design, Instagram and Xiaohongshu activity, commissioned pieces, direct customer conversations, and live demonstrations.

Building the experience

  • Built and managed a handmade calligraphy brand through social media, product development, and customer engagement.
  • Managed Instagram and Xiaohongshu content to promote products and build brand awareness.
  • Developed and presented products including Chinese couplets, bookmarks, framed artwork, and seasonal collections.
  • Conducted live calligraphy demonstrations and workshops for customer engagement at Lenovo Malaysia and EcoWorld sales galleries.

Badminton stories shaped for short-form viewing

I edited badminton moments for Douyin with a focus on pace, emotion, and fan appeal. The project developed my ability to select strong moments, build momentum quickly, and package a familiar sport into content people wanted to watch and share.

A video published on 5 August 2022 reached 362,500 plays and 28,300 likes.

Lenovo live calligraphy activation

For Lenovo Malaysia, I helped turn traditional calligraphy into an engaging live activity. I prepared the visual setup, demonstrated the craft in real time, guided participants through the process, and helped create a workshop atmosphere that felt both organized and welcoming.

The activation brought together live craft, participant interaction, and a finished display that made the experience visible throughout the event.

Fragrance retail and booth promotion

I supported Brēth & Co. through intermittent part-time promoter shifts from 2024 to 2026, most recently in August 2026, and worked with The Perfume Hub in 2024. Across both roles, I turned product knowledge into friendly, useful customer conversations.

Customer experience approach
